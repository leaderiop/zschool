## 1. Purpose, scope, and method

This file is the PRD's register for tracking uncertainty. It covers:

1. Tracking hypotheses **H-14 to H-22** (points still to verify against primary texts, `PROJECT.md` §16.2), with their status as of 09/09/2026, what remains open, and a suggested action (owner, deadline); it also includes follow-up actions on version-1.0 hypotheses that require field validation (H-12, to be tested with pilots).
2. A summary of the **20 divergences** found between the `PROJECT.md` v1.1 baseline and the state of the law and market as of 09/09/2026 (recorded in full in `prd/research/00-baseline-corrections.md`), their treatment in the PRD, and the baseline-update requests to submit to the founder (`PROJECT.md` §18: any change goes through a new version of the document).
3. The **consolidation of every open question** in the PRD: those already raised in `prd/journeys/00-journey-map.md` (OQ-01, OQ-02) and `prd/03-domain-data-model.md` (OQ-01 to OQ-05), and the new questions raised by research, carried by this file.
4. The regulatory and contractual **monitoring process** that keeps this register current after the review.

**Method.** Verification was done via web research on 09/09/2026, recorded with sources in `prd/research/01` to `prd/research/05`; every external fact cited here is referenced there. The baseline remains frozen: where research corrects it, the PRD uses the up-to-date data, cites `prd/research/00-baseline-corrections.md`, and the divergence is recorded as an open question (OQ-07).

**Cross-reference conventions.** Chapters still being drafted are identified by their directory and module code (conventions §2): for example "the FIN module (`prd/modules/16-finance-billing-collections.md`)" or "`prd/cross-cutting/35-external-integrations.md` (integrations)." Final file numbering and the consolidated matrix are generated in `prd/README.md` at the end of the review. No requirement identifier from an unwritten file is cited here.

### 5.4 Update workflow

1. A monitoring finding is timestamped and sourced in `prd/research/` (a new file or a dated addendum), then summarized in this file (the H table in §2, the §3.1 corrections, the §4 open questions).
2. If the finding changes an already-written requirement: the requirement's owning file is amended, citing the `prd/research/` cross-reference; if the finding contradicts the baseline: an update request goes to the founder per `PROJECT.md` §18 (OQ-07), with no unilateral change.
3. Dated triggers are reviewed in committee: 20/09/2026 (the UTC+0 switch), 01/10/2026 (the WhatsApp rate card), Q4 2026 (the OCI catalogue, CNDP filings before 15/12/2026) and 01/02/2027 (pilot activation: H-12, H-16), Law 59.21 decree publications (H-14, OQ-03, OQ-04), before the 2027 commercial campaign (H-22, OQ-05).
