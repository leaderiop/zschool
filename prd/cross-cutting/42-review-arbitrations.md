### 1.2 Identity, accounts, guardians

| ID | Decision | Rationale | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| ARB-07 | **Login identifier distinct from the contact identifier.** `User` carries a *login identifier* (by default the mobile number, unique) and *contact identifiers* (mobile, email). A **minor student with no mobile number of their own** receives a platform-generated login identifier (readable, not derived from the Massar code to avoid enumeration), activated by a legal guardian with an OTP sent to that guardian's number; a tracked migration to a personal number is possible at any time (once the student reaches adulthood). **Two guardians sharing one mobile number**: the second receives a generated identifier, with the OTP routed to the household number declared as a shared contact; INV-13 (one account per person) is unchanged. **Foreign numbers** are accepted in E.164 international format; SMS falls back to an international gateway, WhatsApp is preferred, cost charged per FR-COM-08. New invariant INV-45. Resolves `journeys/07` OQ-06. | RG-01, RG-12b, DEC-11, and INV-37 are incompatible without this distinction. | Applied |
| ARB-08 | **Number change, loss, and reassignment.** A new SEC requirement (MVP): (a) self-service change via OTP to both the old and the new number; (b) otherwise, at the front desk of a school with an active relationship: identity verification by the office staff, approval by leadership, logged, with other schools notified; (c) recycled-number detection: after 6 months with no login, or after repeated OTP failures, a knowledge challenge (a linked child's date of birth, entered without being displayed) is required before any access, with schools alerted; (d) *claim* (FR-INS-08): the same knowledge challenge, with a school able to revoke a *claim* and reissue it. | High SIM churn; a recycled number would otherwise grant access to another family's data. | Applied |

### 1.3 Student life, assessments, documents, finance, communication

| ID | Decision | Rationale | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| ARB-15 | **Absence notification.** (a) Only the **first absence of the day** (or of the half-day, configurable per cycle) triggers the under-5-minute notification; later absences on the same day are grouped into an evening summary. (b) A **3-minute hold window** after validation, during which a correction cancels the send; after sending, any correction triggers a **correction notice** on the same channel. (c) The **quiet-hours window** (FR-VSC-06) never applies to attendance notifications; it defers other notifications only. (d) **Front-desk justification**: student-life staff may record a justification on a guardian's behalf (a scanned paper document or a verbal reason), logged as "entered by the school". (e) A discrepancy between two roll calls for the same session (teacher / supervisor): the first validation stands for notification purposes; the second opens a discrepancy for student-life staff to arbitrate, with no further notification while it stays open. (f) Preschool: delay and grouping configurable per cycle (preschool default: half-day summary). | §10 (5 minutes), the cost of consumables, the reality of false positives. | Applied |
| ARB-16 | **Temporary exclusion and SUSPENDED.** A "temporary exclusion" sanction (FR-VSC-15) creates `AttendanceRecord` entries of type "exclusion (administrative absence)" for its duration, with no absence notification and no effect on attendance-rate counts. | ARB-17 | **Grading calculation rules (MVP).** (a) Markers: an unjustified absence defaults to a grade of 0 (configurable: 0 or excluded); a justified absence, an exemption, or "not assessed" are excluded from the denominator. (d) A mid-period class change: grades stay attached to the enrollment; rank is computed within the current class at closing. (e) Exclusion from rank: an indicator on `PeriodResult`, carried by FR-EVA-09 (FR-VSC-22 points to it). (f) Progressive grade publication (FR-EVA-19): **MVP**, configurable per school, with a "draft / published" status on `Mark` (a new attribute); the default is publication at closing. (g) FR-EVA-17: any correction to a published report card creates a new version, with no exception and no grace period. (i) A year-end decision of "not yet determined" for certifying grade levels, updated once results are imported (FR-MAS-10), before the INV-25 grace period ends. (j) "Local unified exam": a single internal `Assessment` type (FR-EVA-01); FR-EVA-10 covers only external exams (provincial, regional, national). | Underpins every MVP average. | Applied |

(b) **Cancelling a payment or a receipt**: a new FR-FIN requirement (MVP) via a logged counter-entry (reason, approval, the original number preserved, a numbered cancellation receipt). (c) **Proration**: a started month is due in full by default (observed practice), with an optional daily-proration setting; applies to arrivals, departures, and transfers; `UsageMetric` counts a transferred student only once, in the transfer month, at the originating school. (d) **Re-enrollment** is never a conditionable service (CNF-14 is authoritative; FR-DOC-09 corrected). (c) **Per-person language**: an FR/AR preference on `User`, applied to SMS, WhatsApp, and in-app; an SMS is sent only in the recipient's language; bilingual institutional content (FR-COM-06) covers announcements, documents, and in-app messages. ### 1.4 Transfers, careers, reporting

| ID | Decision | Rationale | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| ARB-22 | **Transfer.** `TransferRequest` statuses: initiated, approved by the origin school, accepted by the destination school, activated (the origin's closing and the destination's activation happen in one transaction), **declined** (by the destination school only, for reasons of capacity, an unauthorized track, or an incomplete file), **cancelled** (by the legal tutor or the adult student before activation), **expired** (30 days after approval with no activation: the origin school stays ACTIVE, both parties are notified). | ARB-23 | **Teachers.** (a) FR-CAR-12: the cross-school hour total is visible only to the teacher; a school sees only its own hours and, if the teacher has consented to share the total, a binary alert ("cap exceeded across all affiliations"); hours outside the platform (public sector, non-ZSchool schools) are self-declared. (b) FR-MAS-08 and FR-RAP: qualifications exported to ESISE are those declared to the employer on `SchoolMembership` (a declared qualification), never the `TeacherProfile`. (e) A scheduled future affiliation end date is allowed, with course reassignment before that date. | RG-17, RG-20, RG-35, INV-17, INV-29. | Applied |
| ARB-24 | **Reporting and capacity.** (a) **Attendance rate** = (expected sessions − unjustified absences − justified absences) / expected sessions, computed over declared sessions (ARB-04); a school may also display the rate excluding justified absences; a single definition lives in FR-RAP-01 and is reused by KPI-06/07. (b) **Capacity**: per-class capacity (FR-PED-02) checked at placement, with leadership approval required to exceed it; per-grade-level capacity (FR-INS-04) = the sum of that level's class capacities. (c) **Effective date** of an enrollment: attendance rates, thresholds, and exports count from the effective date. (d) ESISE: FR-RAP-11 prepares and checks the data, FR-MAS-07..09 produce the files; there is a single control view (FR-RAP-11).
