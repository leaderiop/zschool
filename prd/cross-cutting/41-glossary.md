## 1. Purpose and glossary conventions

### 1.1 Purpose

This chapter is the **PRD's terminology reference** (`prd/00-conventions.md` §1.1): it carries over the entire baseline glossary (`PROJECT.md` §17, 41 lines, with compound entries broken out) by **rewording every definition from a ZSchool perspective**, then extends it with business, product, integration, regulatory, and drafting terms used throughout the chapters. It aims for a single vocabulary: one concept = one entry; variants and synonyms are listed inside the relevant entry and must not be used loosely elsewhere.

The glossary stays trilingual by design: this is a Morocco-focused product whose own user interface is French/Arabic bilingual (DEC-10), and Moroccan law and Massar terminology are French/Arabic source terms. The **French term** column therefore keeps the actual term used in the product, in Moroccan law, and by Massar; the **Arabic term** column keeps the official or field-standard Arabic wording; the **English term** column gives the working English equivalent used across this now-English PRD; and the **ZSchool definition** column is written in English.

### 1.2 Entry structure

Each entry carries five columns:

- **French term**: the term used in the product and in Moroccan sources; accepted variants appear in parentheses.
- **Arabic term**: Arabic script. Official Moroccan terminology (statutes, ministry usage) takes priority; failing that, common sector usage. Acronyms with no settled Arabic translation stay in Latin script.
- **English term**: the working equivalent used in this PRD (per `prd/00-conventions.md` §1.1, foreign terms are set in italics in running text).
- **ZSchool definition**: the meaning adopted in the PRD — an operational definition, not an exhaustive legal one. It never contradicts the baseline; where research corrected the baseline, the up-to-date fact is used and the divergence is logged under "Open questions" (§7).
- **Cross-references**: PRD files or baseline sections to consult (file paths, per `prd/00-conventions.md` §5).

### 1.3 Usage rules across chapters

1. **First occurrence** of a term in a chapter: the French term, optionally followed by the Arabic or English equivalent in italics in parentheses. Later occurrences: the French term alone.
2. **Parental qualities** (RG-14b): use "legal guardian" and "custodial parent" as **distinct, independent** qualities; never merge the two notions (see §2.1 and OQ-02 of `prd/research/00-baseline-corrections.md`).
3. **Versions and priorities**: MVP/V1/V2+ and Must/Should/Could tags point to `PROJECT.md` §12 and §6; their use is normative in every requirement table.
4. **Acronyms**: on first occurrence in a chapter, spell out the acronym (for example "AREF (*Regional Academy of Education and Training*)"), then use the acronym alone.
