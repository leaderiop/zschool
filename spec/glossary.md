# ZSchool — Glossary

This document is the **spec's terminology reference** (`spec/process/requirement-id-scheme.md` §1): it carries over the entire baseline glossary by rewording every definition from a ZSchool perspective, then extends it with business, product, integration, regulatory, and drafting terms used throughout the spec. It aims for a single vocabulary: one concept = one entry; variants and synonyms are listed inside the relevant entry and must not be used loosely elsewhere.

The glossary stays trilingual by design: this is a Morocco-focused product whose own user interface is French/Arabic bilingual (ADR-ZS-010), and Moroccan law and Massar terminology are French/Arabic source terms. The **French term** column therefore keeps the actual term used in the product, in Moroccan law, and by Massar; the **Arabic term** column keeps the official or field-standard Arabic wording; the **English term** column gives the working English equivalent used across the spec; and the **ZSchool definition** column is written in English.

## 1. Purpose and glossary conventions

### 1.1 Entry structure

Each entry carries five columns:

- **French term**: the term used in the product and in Moroccan sources; accepted variants appear in parentheses.
- **Arabic term**: Arabic script. Official Moroccan terminology (statutes, ministry usage) takes priority; failing that, common sector usage. Acronyms with no settled Arabic translation stay in Latin script.
- **English term**: the working equivalent used in the spec (foreign terms are set in italics in running text).
- **ZSchool definition**: the meaning adopted in the spec — an operational definition, not an exhaustive legal one.
- **Cross-references**: spec files or historical baseline sections to consult.

### 1.2 Usage rules across chapters

1. **First occurrence** of a term in a chapter: the French term, optionally followed by the Arabic or English equivalent in italics in parentheses. Later occurrences: the French term alone.
2. **Parental qualities** (INV-ZS-066): use "legal guardian" and "custodial parent" as **distinct, independent** qualities; never merge the two notions (see §2.1).
3. **Versions and priorities**: MVP/V1/V2+ and Must/Should/Could tags are normative in every requirement entry.
4. **Acronyms**: on first occurrence in a chapter, spell out the acronym (for example "AREF (*Regional Academy of Education and Training*)"), then use the acronym alone.
