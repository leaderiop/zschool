# Journey: Fatima, Primary School Secretary-Cashier

## 1. Purpose and scope

This file describes Fatima's daily journeys: secretary-cashier at a 350-student primary school in Salé, she enrolls, collects payments, prints certificates, and answers the phone, on an old PC and her mobile phone. Steps are identified `JNY-ZS-NNN` and link to the critical journeys `JMP-ZS-NNN` in the map `spec/journeys/00-journey-map.md`.

In scope:

1. Enrolling a new student at the front desk (JNY-ZS-011).
2. Collecting a monthly payment in cash with a cash session (JNY-ZS-012).
3. Collecting start-of-year cheques and tracking bounces (JNY-ZS-013).
4. Issuing and delivering certificates and attestations (JNY-ZS-014).
5. Updating a student's file: missing documents and identity correction (JNY-ZS-015).
6. Phone reminder for an unpaid fee with an account statement (JNY-ZS-016).
7. Responding to a parent: announcements, summons, messages (JNY-ZS-017).
8. Re-enrollment and year-end rollover, secretariat side (JNY-ZS-018).
9. Voiding or correcting a payment and a receipt (JNY-ZS-019).
10. Arrival of a student from a school outside ZSchool (JNY-ZS-020).
11. Mid-year resumption: importing payment schedules, cheques, and grades from a semester in progress (JNY-ZS-021).

Out of scope (handled elsewhere): the admission decision, tests, and waitlist (owned by leadership, JNY-ZS-008 of `spec/journeys/01-school-group-director.md`, V1), a parent claiming their account (`spec/journeys/05-multi-school-parent.md`, JMP-ZS-004), taking roll call (`spec/journeys/03-head-supervisor.md`, JMP-ZS-005), grade entry (`spec/journeys/04-part-time-teacher.md`, JMP-ZS-006), online payment by the parent (`spec/journeys/05-multi-school-parent.md`, JMP-ZS-008), the full transfer procedure (JMP-ZS-009, owned by leadership), and school configuration (JMP-ZS-003, owned by leadership; Fatima only reviews imports). Functional requirements and `ECR-…` screens are carried by the module chapters `spec/behaviors/02-admissions-enrollment-reenrollment.md`, `spec/behaviors/06-documents-certificates.md`, `spec/behaviors/07-finance-billing-collections.md`, `spec/behaviors/08-communication-notifications.md`; this file describes the journeys without duplicating the requirement templates.

## 5. Gains over the paper process

The table compares the persona's current practice (paper registers, Excel files, handwritten notes — §2.1, §2.11) with the target behavior described above. The gains are qualitative: the program's quantified indicators (collection rate, notification delay, reduction in double entry) are carried by `spec/metrics.md` and not invented here.

| Task                                      | Current paper process                                                                                            | With ZSchool                                                                                                                                           | Expected gains                                                                       |
| ----------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Enrollment (JNY-ZS-011)                   | A paper form then re-entered into a spreadsheet and into Massar; a risk of duplicates; identifiers noted by hand | One pass at the front desk: search by phone or Massar code, anti-duplicate matching, contract generated from the fee schedule, invitation by SMS       | Zero re-entry, duplicates avoided at the source, a usable file from the first minute |
| Cash payment (JNY-ZS-012)                 | A handwritten cash register, receipt stubs, totals recounted in the evening, discrepancies found late            | Collection in a few steps, a numbered receipt handed over and sent, a real-time cash log, a discrepancy computed at closing                            | An accurate, defensible cash drawer, a receipt always given, no more manual recounts |
| Cheques (JNY-ZS-013)                      | Cheque due dates tracked in a notebook; bounces discovered at the bank with no link to the file                  | A full lifecycle: due dates scheduled, reminders before deposit, a bounce linked to the installment and the reminder                                   | No cheque forgotten, bounces handled with the history in view                        |
| Certificates (JNY-ZS-014)                 | Word templates retyped, a manual seal, no trace of requests, tension when arrears exist                          | Issuance in seconds, bilingual, numbered, sealed, and QR-coded, an arrears alert with no blocking, a statement attached, requests and issuances logged | Compliance (ADR-ZS-005, Law 59.21), no more retyping, written proof in a dispute     |
| Student file (JNY-ZS-015)                 | Documents in cardboard folders, missing items noted nowhere, corrections never propagated                        | A completeness list per student, reminders to families, identity corrections historized and notified to other schools                                  | A verifiably complete file, reliable identity platform-wide                          |
| Reminders (JNY-ZS-016)                    | Calls from numbers noted on paper, promises not tracked, a sometimes confrontational tone                        | An up-to-date record, a statement sent during the call, a dated promise, configured escalation, the ADR-ZS-005 safeguard                               | Shorter, less tense calls, tracked commitments, no illegal threats                   |
| Responding to parents (JNY-ZS-017)        | Responses from memory or after checking three registers; summons on paper distributed by the student             | An up-to-date front-desk record, bilingual templates, a summons with read tracking                                                                     | An accurate response the first time, proof of distribution                           |
| Re-enrollment and year end (JNY-ZS-018)   | Paper re-enrollment lists, deposits noted by hand, N+1 classes recopied in a spreadsheet                         | A pre-filled campaign, a progress table, tracked deposits, an automatic rollover on the cloned structure                                               | Retention tracked class by class, year N+1 ready with no re-entry                    |
| Correcting a receipt (JNY-ZS-019)         | A crossed-out receipt, a scratched-out stub, an unexplained cash discrepancy                                     | A tracked reversal and a numbered voiding receipt                                                                                                      | Tamper-proof numbering and a defensible cash drawer                                  |
| Arrival from outside ZSchool (JNY-ZS-020) | The student's history unknown or recopied from memory                                                            | A declared prior history with documents, no duplicate identity                                                                                         | Statistics and future transfers fed with data                                        |
| Mid-year resumption (JNY-ZS-021)          | Re-entering five months of payments and grades                                                                   | A checked import of payment schedules, cheques, and grades                                                                                             | Go-live with no re-entry or undue reminder                                           |
