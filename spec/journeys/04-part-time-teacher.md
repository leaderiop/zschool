# Journey: Khadija, Part-Time Teacher

## 1. Purpose and scope

This file describes the end-to-end journeys of **Khadija, a part-time math teacher** at two private schools in Rabat: first activating her account, day-to-day handling of the multi-school context, taking roll call for her courses, entering grades in the evening on mobile, the lesson log and homework, messages to her students' parents, preparing her averages before the council, and managing her professional profile. The "public-sector outside teacher" status journey (AREF authorization, 8-hour cap) is carried by a secondary character, **Hassan, a part-time public-sector teacher at School A**, since Khadija is a private-sector part-timer whom this cap does not concern (ADR-ZS-064).

Steps are identified `JNY-ZS-NNN` and link to the critical journeys `JMP-ZS-NNN` in the map `spec/journeys/00-journey-map.md`.

In scope:

1. First activation: invitation, account creation, claiming an affiliation (JNY-ZS-041).
2. The daily multi-school context switcher (JNY-ZS-042).
3. Taking roll call for her courses on mobile, including offline (JNY-ZS-043).
4. Evening grade entry on mobile: saving, leadership lock (JNY-ZS-044).
5. Lesson log and homework (JNY-ZS-045).
6. Messages to her students' parents, moderated mode (JNY-ZS-046).
7. Preparing her averages before the class council (JNY-ZS-047).
8. Professional profile and verified experience (JNY-ZS-048).
9. Availability and discreet applications on the teacher network (JNY-ZS-049).
10. AREF authorization for a "public-sector outside teacher": the 8-hour cap and documents — secondary character Hassan, not Khadija (JNY-ZS-050).

Persona needs (`URS-ZS-026..033`) are carried by `spec/urs.md`; this file narrates them without redefining them. Functional requirements and screens are carried by the module chapters `spec/behaviors/01-administration-onboarding-subscription.md`, `spec/behaviors/03-academic-structure-timetables.md`, `spec/behaviors/04-attendance-student-life-discipline.md`, `spec/behaviors/05-assessments-grades-report-cards.md`, `spec/behaviors/08-communication-notifications.md`, `spec/behaviors/10-teacher-career-network.md`, `spec/behaviors/11-dashboards-reporting.md`, `spec/behaviors/12-massar-regulatory-exports.md`; this file describes the journeys without duplicating the requirement templates.

## 2. Usage profile and persona constraints

Constraints that shape this file's journeys:

1. **Multi-affiliation**: two Rabat schools in the same year; roughly half of private-sector teachers are in this situation, per an industry source (`spec/appendices/01-review-history.md`, research notes — a discrepancy with the baseline's "over 50%" is logged there too, OQ-ZS-221).
2. **Mobile-first, unstable network**: not always a computer in class; roll call and grade entry on the phone, with resume after an outage. Per the ANRT digital-usage survey cited in `spec/appendices/01-review-history.md` (research notes), the large majority of individuals aged 5 and over use the internet and own a smartphone; WhatsApp dominates social-media usage (a wording discrepancy with the baseline is logged there too, OQ-ZS-225).
3. **Evening entry**: grades are entered in the evening, on a limited data plan; write timestamps are expressed in the `Africa/Casablanca` time zone, now **permanent UTC+0** from 20/09/2026 (a discrepancy with the baseline is logged in `spec/appendices/01-review-history.md`, OQ-ZS-224).
4. **Career confidentiality**: Khadija is looking for a full-time position next year; her search must not leak to her current employers.
5. **Free of charge**: the teacher pays nothing; the paying customer is the school (ADR-ZS-024).

## 5. Rules cutting across the persona

1. **One account, several contexts**: Khadija's account is unique and global; profiles and affiliations combine with no data merging (INV-ZS-053, INV-ZS-069, INV-ZS-088, ADR-ZS-014, INV-ZS-030, INV-ZS-009, INV-ZS-040; `spec/cross-cutting/01-permissions.md`).
2. **Systematic least privilege**: in every context, visibility derives from active assignments; every write is logged with author, context, and timestamp (INV-ZS-090, INV-ZS-091, INV-ZS-019).
3. **Mobile and offline first**: roll call, grade entry, and the lesson log run as a PWA with outage tolerance and sync; typical pages under 2 seconds on 4G (`spec/cross-cutting/03-non-functional-requirements.md`; `spec/cross-cutting/05-ux-ui-mobile-first-rtl.md`). Native apps in V2.
4. **Bilingualism**: interface and content in French and Arabic with full RTL, names in both scripts (ADR-ZS-021).
5. **Mobile contact**: the phone number is the contact identifier; e-mail is optional and never required (ADR-ZS-022, INV-ZS-044).
6. **Notification channel**: in MVP, in-app, WhatsApp utility limited to attendance notifications for opted-in parents, an SMS aggregator as a fallback, with no push; in V1, push first, then general WhatsApp utility, then SMS (ADR-ZS-023, ADR-ZS-036, ADR-ZS-062; `spec/behaviors/08-communication-notifications.md`).
7. **The teacher pays nothing**: no feature described here is billed to Khadija (ADR-ZS-024).
8. **What history means**: when an affiliation closes, access disappears, author traces stay (INV-ZS-071, INV-ZS-010); the professional profile follows Khadija (INV-ZS-072, INV-ZS-087).
