# ADR-ZS-008: Reference growth ambition and horizon

> **Status:** Accepted
> **Date:** 2026-09-09
> **Historical aliases:** DEC-27

## Context

A commercial ambition figure is load-bearing well beyond sales planning: non-
functional sizing (target scale, chapter 10), infrastructure capacity planning, and
pricing viability all need a concrete number to design against, not an open-ended
"as many schools as possible." The figure needed the product owner's direct sign-off
since it commits the roadmap to a specific pace.

## Decision

Reference ambition: 20 schools and 15,000 students by the end of year 1 (pilots,
then the Casablanca–Kénitra corridor), 300 schools and 200,000 students at three
years. Confirmed by the product owner on 9 September 2026.

## Consequences

**Positive**: gives every downstream sizing decision (chapter 10's "500 schools,
500,000 students" technical target, sized at roughly 2.5× this figure) a concrete
number to design against instead of an open-ended guess.

**Negative**: if actual adoption diverges significantly from this figure in either
direction, every piece of sizing work anchored to it (infrastructure capacity,
pricing-model viability, staffing plans) needs revisiting.

**Trade-off accepted**: a firm reference number, explicitly confirmed by the product
owner rather than left as a range, over the flexibility of an unstated target —
because unsized infrastructure and pricing decisions can't actually be made without
committing to some number.
