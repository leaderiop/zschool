# ADR-ZS-059: The 6AP exam is labeled "provincial," correcting a research-file divergence

> **Status:** Accepted
> **Date:** 2026-09-09
> **Historical aliases:** ARB-18

## Context

The baseline document (§2.2) and a research file (`research/04`) disagreed on
whether the standardized primary-studies-certificate exam is "provincial" or
"regional." The baseline reflects established practice; the research finding is a
single divergent data point without the order reference number needed to verify it
independently.

## Decision

The PRD keeps "provincial exam," matching the baseline's established-practice
reading, and flags the research file's "regional" reading as a divergence to be
resolved once the order's reference number is obtained (tracked as H-16).

## Consequences

**Positive**: keeps the PRD consistent with the baseline's authoritative reading
rather than silently adopting an unverified single-source divergence.

**Negative**: the discrepancy stays genuinely unresolved until the order reference
is actually located — this decision picks a default, not a verified answer.

**Trade-off accepted**: trusting the baseline as the more authoritative source over
the research finding, while explicitly flagging (not hiding) the divergence for
future verification.
