# ADR-ZS-030: Market positioning and primary target segment

> **Status:** Accepted
> **Date:** 2026-09-09
> **Historical aliases:** DEC-19

## Context

A platform this broad could plausibly target anywhere from tiny single-site schools
to the largest multi-site groups; without a stated primary segment, product,
sales, and infrastructure priorities would each end up guessing independently at
who the platform is really built for first.

## Decision

Positioning centers on a multi-school global identity, a reliable parent app,
Fatourati-based collections, and a bilingual FR/AR interface. The primary target is
schools of 300 to 3,000 students along the Casablanca–Kénitra corridor, home to 70%
of Morocco's private schools.

## Consequences

**Positive**: gives sales, onboarding design, and infrastructure sizing a concrete
segment to optimize for, instead of trying to serve the smallest neighborhood school
and the largest national group equally well from day one.

**Negative**: the tiny single-site school (under 300 students) and the very largest
groups (over 3,000) are both secondary, and onboarding/pricing may fit them less
well until the product matures beyond this primary segment.

**Trade-off accepted**: a focused primary segment, chosen for corridor density and
scale fit, over trying to be equally optimized for every school size and region from
launch.
