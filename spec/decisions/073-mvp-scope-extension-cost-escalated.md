# ADR-ZS-073: Confirming the MVP scope extension and its development cost is escalated

> **Status:** Escalated — pending product owner sign-off
> **Date:** 2026-09-09
> **Historical aliases:** ESC-02

## Context

ADR-ZS-041 already records the substance of the MVP scope extension (covering a
full mid-year close) and marks itself "working hypothesis." This entry is the
formal escalation record for getting the product owner to confirm both that the
extension itself is correct and that its added development-effort cost is
acceptable before the February 2027 activation date it's meant to support.

## Decision

Not yet decided. See ADR-ZS-041 for the scope in question; this ADR tracks its
escalated status and the outstanding cost-confirmation ask specifically.

## Consequences

**Positive**: none yet.

**Negative**: the wave-1/wave-2 requirement set (ADR-ZS-041) proceeds on a working
hypothesis until the product owner confirms both the scope and its cost are
acceptable ahead of the February 2027 pilot activation date.

**Trade-off accepted**: none — this is an open decision, not yet made.
